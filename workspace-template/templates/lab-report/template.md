# {{title}}

**Aim** — one sentence: what is being measured, and by what method.

**Apparatus** — every instrument with its least count and range. A reading is only as good as the instrument's smallest division, and saying so here is what makes the error analysis later honest.

**Theory** — the relation being tested, each symbol defined, and the rearranged form that the graph will plot. If the experiment involves a slope, write the slope's meaning now.

**Procedure** — numbered, past tense, short sentences. Include the observations that were checked for consistency (repeat readings, reversal, zero correction). Nothing about what was *supposed* to happen.

**Observations** — the tables, with units in the headers and the readings as recorded. Every calculated column says how it was calculated.

**Graph** — axis labels with units, the chosen scales, the best-fit line, the slope with its units, and the intercept read from the graph.

**Calculations** — one worked substitution with the numbers in place, then the result with its uncertainty. Keep the algebra before the arithmetic: `Y = (4 M g L) / (π r² l)`, then the values.

**Error analysis** — instrument error, the spread of repeats, and the largest contribution named. Say which one dominates and why; a percentage uncertainty on the result follows from the slope's uncertainty, not from intuition.

**Result** — the value, its uncertainty, and the accepted value with the difference. If the difference is larger than the uncertainty, say what most likely caused it.

**Precautions and sources of error** — the ones this experiment actually had, not a generic list.
