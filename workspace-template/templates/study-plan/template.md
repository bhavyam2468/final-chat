**Rules that keep it alive**
- One topic per slot. Splitting an hour across three topics feels productive and leaves nothing learned.
- Practice after reading, never instead of it — and the practice is what the review slot is for.
- A missed slot moves to the review slot, it does not stack onto the next day.
- The last day before the test is revision only. New material the night before buys anxiety, not marks.
